Frequently Asked Questions



How do I book a tour?

You can book a tour through our website, by selecting a tour and filling out the booking form.

What should I bring on the tour?

We recommend wearing comfortable shoes, clothing appropriate for the weather, and safety equipment.

Are the tours available in different languages?

Yes, our guides can lead tours in different languages. Please let us know your preference when booking.

Should we overcome unexpected situations, like a flat tire, on our own?

Of course not, we are a team and we always help each other, we are here to overcome any problem.

Do I have to pay to participate in your team?

No way, we do not ask for money from our members, just the desire and motivation for cycling.

Do I need to have a professional bike?

You can use any bike that is upright and can guarantee a successful bike tour.
